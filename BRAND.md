# Benchnote — Brand identity

**Slug:** `benchnote` · **Tagline:** A listing, photo, and launch cadence for handmade sellers.
**Status:** Live. Payments processed by Gumroad (Monthly or Quarterly).

## Positioning
Side-hustle and full-time makers selling on Etsy, their own site, or at markets.

**Promise (honest):** Planning help only. No sales, ranking, or income promises.

## Personas
- **New maker:** Ready to open, not sure what to list first.
- **Weekend seller:** A day job and a shop that goes quiet for weeks.
- **Full-time maker:** Busy making, behind on listings and launches.

## Customer journey the landing page follows
1. **Make** — pain: Selling tasks eat making time. → plan: Make days plus one shop-desk block.
2. **Photograph** — pain: Photos look different every time. → plan: One window, one backdrop, one checklist.
3. **List** — pain: Listings go up in random bursts. → plan: A weekly batch day and reusable templates.
4. **Launch** — pain: Collections drop with no plan. → plan: A 4-week countdown — no fake scarcity.

## Voice & tone
Encouraging studio-mate. Hands-on, a little craft-y, never salesy.
- Short, concrete sentences. Checklists over hype.
- Never shame, scare, or invent urgency.

## Logo
`static/logo.svg` — monogram mark on a rounded tile in the primary accent. Minimum size 24px; clear space ≈ 25% of the mark height. Use on light backgrounds; on dark, keep the tile as-is.

## Pricing
- **Monthly** — $19 / month. Renews automatically. Cancel anytime from your Gumroad library.
- **Quarterly** — $49 every 3 months. Renews automatically. Cancel anytime from your Gumroad library.

## Do
- Celebrate the craft; respect making time.
- Show the pricing worksheet as a tool, not advice.
- Show Monthly and Quarterly prices, plus the renewal sentence, before checkout.
- Save the customer’s email before sending them to Gumroad.
- Keep the disclaimer visible near every plan.

## Don’t
- Show income screenshots or earnings claims (FTC).
- Use fake scarcity in launch copy.
- Fake testimonials, user counts, stats, or press logos.
- Countdown timers, “spots left”, or other urgency/scare copy.
